About Us

Welcome! This website allows students to have the freedom to choose which organizations they want to join based on their interests and desire to explore new opportunities. As students, we all go through the stages of being freshmen, sophomores, juniors, and seniors. At first, we may be unfamiliar with the organizations in our university, but as we get older, it is too late to join organizations because of our workloads and personal stuff.

We are students from the Computer Engineering Technology program, and we’ve created a website to help you explore various non-college organizations you can join. The website provides a list of organizations, along with their respective president and the projects they have completed. You can browse through the organizations that interest you and find details about membership opportunities, including joining dates and interview schedules.
